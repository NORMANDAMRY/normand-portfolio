# My 18-Week Internship at Consurv Technic

From February to July 2026, I had the opportunity to intern at **Consurv Technic Sdn Bhd** as an IT Engineer. Looking back, it was an incredible journey where I built **5 production-grade systems** from scratch.

## The Projects

### Consurv Saudi Corporate Website
The first major project was building [consurv.sa](https://consurv.sa) — a fully bilingual (English/Arabic) B2B corporate website. This was my first deep dive into RTL (Right-to-Left) support, and I learned a ton about:

- Arabic typography and layout considerations
- Responsive design across 11 sections
- Achieving 90+ Lighthouse scores

### HRApp
Next up was HRApp, a cross-platform HR mobile application. With 11+ modules and 242 commits, this was the most feature-rich app I've built. Key features included:

- QR code attendance check-in
- Leave management and approvals
- Medical claims
- Business card sharing with vCard QR codes
- Offline sync with Room DB

### KrakenAi
Finally, I built KrakenAi — a multi-provider AI assistant. This one was particularly exciting because it involved:

- Integration with 6+ LLM providers
- RAG pipeline with ChromaDB
- Real-time streaming via WebSocket and SSE
- Serverless deployment on Supabase

## Key Takeaways

1. **Version control matters** — 242 commits on HRApp alone taught me the value of incremental development.
2. **Full-stack thinking** — Working across mobile, web, desktop, and serverless platforms gave me a holistic view of software engineering.
3. **User feedback is gold** — UAT sessions and user feedback shaped better products.
4. **Documentation pays off** — The 2-hour training session for the RFQ Manager reminded me that good docs = happy users.

I'm grateful for the mentorship from the Consurv team, especially Puan Syuhada and the engineering team who guided me throughout this journey.
